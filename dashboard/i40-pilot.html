i40 Pilot

What this system is currently validating, how old the evidence is, and what would settle it.

i40 is Industry 4.0. An I4.0 pilot project is a small-scale initiative run to test and validate before committing to full-scale implementation. The industrial half of I4.0 — IoT sensors, cyber-physical plant, predictive maintenance of machines — does not apply to a trading system and is not pretended to here. The pilot discipline does: run it small, measure it, and do not commit money until the evidence clears a stated bar. Roadmap and stage completion live in SYSTEM-MAP.md; this page is live state, not a copy of it. Nothing here feeds the gate.
