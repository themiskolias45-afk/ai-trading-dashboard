The whole record — both boxes
Each box journals its own fills, so every other surface shows half the evidence. This pools them — but only while engine parity says the two boxes run the same code, because two engines admit different trades from identical bars and their fills are then not samples of one thing. When parity is stale or divergent this refuses to pool and says so.
What the learning engine has to work with
Real fills on the left, forgone paper episodes on the right. The engine needs 5 CLOSED trades in ONE bucket before it adjusts anything, and it has 2 in total — the rejection ledger has 26 episodes it is not allowed to use. They are kept apart on purpose: shadow episodes are entries that were never filled, with no spread and no slippage, so they are evidence about geometry and not about execution. Nothing on this panel feeds the gate.
What this system knows, and what it only assumes
Every claim carries its verdict, the evidence behind it, its caveat, and — the part that matters most — what would change the answer. A measurement with no stated falsifier is an opinion with a number attached. Curated in server/evidence_register.js and updated whenever something is measured; the per-gate verdicts underneath come from the rejection ledger and update themselves.
Measurements — when this system wins
Out-of-sample, at the LIVE gate, from the replay the engine actually trades. Sessions are six 4-hour blocks because entries are taken on H4 bars — no trade can open at any other hour. Read the fold count, not the pooled number: with this many cells you should expect one or two to look strong by chance, and this project's bar for changing a gate is 5 of 5 folds.
