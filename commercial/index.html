How it works

Four layers of intelligence.
One decision.

Every trade passes through a rigorous multi-stage process before execution. Most signals are rejected — only the strongest setups are taken.

1

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour charts must all align before a signal is considered. A single conflicting timeframe kills the trade.

2

Technical Confirmation

RSI, EMA 20/50/200, Bollinger Bands, MACD, ATR, and volume must confirm the setup. Volume must be 1.4× above average.

3

Macro Filter

DXY (dollar strength) and VIX (fear index) are checked. High fear or strong dollar conditions reduce confidence — preventing entries into headwinds.

4

Claude AI Approval

Claude Opus — Anthropic's most powerful model — evaluates every potential trade before execution. Only approved trades go to MetaTrader 5.
