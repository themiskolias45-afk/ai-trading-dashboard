2. Performance fee: charge % of profits only. Aligns incentives. Hard to track for retail. Requires managed account structure.
3. Hybrid: small monthly fee + performance fee. Best of both but complex.

For retail SaaS — $149/mo is competitive. For managed accounts — performance fee model makes more sense. Start with SaaS first, add managed accounts later once licensed.
