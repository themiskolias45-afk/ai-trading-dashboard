What does the first sellable version of SmartEntry Commercial look like?

Must have:
- Multi-user support (each customer has their own API key, their own MT5 account)
- Onboarding flow: connect your MT5, set risk level, go live
- Customer dashboard (same as personal but branded, simplified)
- Email alerts when trades fire
- Basic support channel

Nice to have but not MVP:
- Mobile app
- Custom asset selection beyond BTC/Gold/SPY
- Social features (leaderboard, copy trading)
- Advanced backtesting per user

The MVP is essentially: a hosted version of SmartEntry Pro that a customer can connect to their own MT5 account and let it run. I do the AI, they provide the capital.
