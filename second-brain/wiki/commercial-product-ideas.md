# Commercial Product Ideas

tags: commercial, saas, managed-accounts, mifid2, business-model

## Summary

- Two customer types identified: retail traders (SaaS subscription) and managed account investors (fee on profits or monthly retainer).
- SaaS is simpler to build first; managed accounts require MiFID II authorization from the HCMC in Greece.
- Open questions remain on broker partnerships, onboarding UX, and which model to prioritize.

## Full Notes

**Customer Segments**

| Segment | Model | Pricing |
|---|---|---|
| Retail traders | SaaS subscription | $149/mo Trader, $299/mo Pro |
| Managed accounts | Performance fee or retainer | TBD — requires compliance |

**Open Questions**
- Which to build first? SaaS is lower friction; managed accounts require regulatory authorization.
- MiFID II requirements for managing client money in Greece/EU — research needed (see [eu-compliance-notes.md](eu-compliance-notes.md))
- Onboarding flow for retail customers: what does connecting their MT5 look like?
- Broker partnership: required or optional for SaaS model?

**Current View**
- Start with SaaS retail product
- Defer managed accounts until licensing is in place
