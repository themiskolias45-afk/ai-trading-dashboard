# Pricing Strategy

tags: pricing, saas, revenue-model, commercial, managed-accounts

## Summary

- Three pricing models evaluated: pure SaaS subscription ($149/$299/mo), performance fee only, and a hybrid monthly + performance fee.
- Market benchmark: algo trading services charge $100–500/mo; hedge funds charge 2% AUM + 20% profits.
- Recommendation is SaaS first for retail ($149/mo is competitive), then add performance fee for managed accounts once licensed.

## Full Notes

**Models Evaluated**

| Model | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS subscription ($149/$299/mo) | Predictable revenue, easy to scale, no performance dependency | No alignment with customer outcomes |
| Performance fee only | Aligns incentives | Hard to track for retail; requires managed account structure |
| Hybrid (small monthly + performance %) | Best of both worlds | Complex to administer |

**Market Benchmarks**
- Algo trading SaaS services: $100–500/mo range
- Hedge funds: 2% AUM + 20% profit ("2 and 20")

**Current Recommendation**
- Retail SaaS: $149/mo (Trader), $299/mo (Pro) — competitive and simple
- Managed accounts: performance fee model — but only after MiFID II licensing is in place
- Sequence: launch SaaS first, add managed accounts later
