# EU Compliance Notes (MiFID II / HCMC)

tags: compliance, mifid2, hcmc, greece, regulation

## Summary

- Managing other people's money in Greece/EU requires MiFID II authorization from the Hellenic Capital Market Commission (HCMC), with likely minimum capital of EUR 75k.
- Key requirements include an investment firm license (or tied agent status), client suitability assessments, reporting obligations, and GDPR compliance.
- A white-label arrangement under a licensed broker is an alternative path that avoids direct licensing.

## Full Notes

**Regulator**
- Greece: Hellenic Capital Market Commission (HCMC)
- Framework: MiFID II (EU-wide)

**Licensing Requirements (to research further)**
- Investment firm license vs tied agent — two different authorization tracks
- Minimum capital: likely EUR 75,000 for portfolio management authorization
- Client suitability assessments required for each investor
- Reporting obligations to the HCMC
- GDPR compliance for all client data handling

**Alternative: White-Label / Broker Partnership**
- Operate under a licensed broker who holds the MiFID II authorization
- SmartEntry acts as a technology/strategy provider, not a regulated entity
- Requires negotiating a commercial arrangement with a suitable broker
- Less control but faster to market and lower regulatory burden

**Next Step**
- Consult a financial lawyer familiar with Greek/EU financial regulation before committing to managed accounts
