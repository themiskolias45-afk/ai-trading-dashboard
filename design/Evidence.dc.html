Reading the two spellings
GC=F and XAUUSD are the same metal; BTC-USD and BTCUSD the same coin. The engine names a symbol as it saw it — broker feed or fallback feed — and they carry different RSI readings for the same instrument at the same moment. Matching one spelling would have hidden half of each asset's rows.
