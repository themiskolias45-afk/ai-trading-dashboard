The safety proof
Proving it changed nothing
Comparing the live signal feed before and after is not a proof — prices move between the two readings, so any difference is unattributable. The replay runs the same engine over fixed historical bars, which makes the comparison deterministic and the result a fact rather than an impression.
Engine throws: 0, all three runs
The check that matters most. A limit hoisted out of the function throws inside the replay sandbox, and the caller swallows it — that failure has silently erased whole cohorts twice.

Moving the limit moves the trade list. Without this second test a setting that silently does nothing looks exactly like a setting that is safe.
Both tests are needed, and neither is sufficient alone. The first says the change is safe; the second says the change is worth having.
